Business Plan & Growth Strategy

1. Executive Summary

Senevon Games is an online gaming platform designed to provide accessible, engaging and interactive gaming experiences through the web.

The platform allows users to discover and play games, compete with friends from anywhere in the world, participate in multiplayer experiences, improve their rankings and compete through global leaderboards.

The primary objective is to build an accessible gaming ecosystem where entertainment, competition and social interaction come together.

Senevon Games will follow a Freemium business model supported by multiple revenue streams, including advertisements, premium subscriptions, virtual currency purchases and business partnerships.

The long-term vision is to transform Senevon Games from a browser-based gaming platform into a larger digital gaming ecosystem.

2. Business Objectives

The primary objectives are:

1. Build a recognizable online gaming brand.
2. Attract and retain a growing community of gamers.
3. Provide accessible free-to-play gaming experiences.
4. Introduce sustainable monetization through advertisements and premium services.
5. Encourage user engagement through rewards, achievements and competitions.
6. Develop partnerships with businesses, educational institutions and organizations.
7. Establish multiple revenue streams instead of depending on advertising alone.

4. Business Model

Senevon Games will operate through a combination of three primary monetization strategies.

A. Advertising Revenue

Advertisements will generate revenue from free users.

Potential advertising placements:

- Display advertisements.
- Banner advertisements.
- Rewarded advertisements.
- Selected in-game advertising placements.

The objective is to monetize free users without significantly affecting their gaming experience.

B. Virtual Currency / Token System

The platform will operate a virtual currency system.

Users can purchase tokens to access selected premium features and experiences.

Potential uses:

- Premium game access.
- Exclusive gaming experiences.
- Special tournaments.
- Cosmetic upgrades.
- Additional platform features.

The token system will provide an additional revenue stream while encouraging continued platform engagement.

C. Subscription Revenue

Senevon Games will introduce a premium subscription service.

Proposed subscription: Senevon Plus

Illustrative pricing:

- Monthly: ₹49
- Yearly: ₹399

Potential benefits:

1. Advertisement-free experience.
2. Monthly premium token allowance.
3. Access to exclusive games.
4. Daily bonus rewards.
5. Exclusive profile customization.
6. Access to special gaming events.
7. Early access to selected features.

The subscription will be optional, allowing free users to continue enjoying the platform.

5. Virtual Currency & Reward Economy

To improve user engagement, the platform can introduce two separate virtual currencies.

Free Coins

Users earn free coins through platform activities.

Examples:

- Daily login.
- Completing daily challenges.
- Playing selected games.
- Unlocking achievements.
- Participating in events.
- Referring new users.

Premium Tokens

Premium tokens are purchased using real money.

They can be used for:

- Premium game access.
- Exclusive experiences.
- Special tournaments.
- Cosmetic items and customization.

Keeping free coins and premium tokens separate will help establish a balanced virtual economy.

The reward system should encourage engagement without forcing users to make purchases.

6. User Engagement & Retention Strategy

Acquiring users is only the first step. The platform must provide reasons for users to return.

Senevon Games will focus on building an engaging gaming community.

Proposed Engagement Features

Daily Rewards

Users receive rewards for returning to the platform.

Achievements

Players unlock achievements based on their gaming activities.

Global Leaderboards

Players compete with users worldwide.

Friend Challenges

Users challenge friends and compare their performance.

Weekly Competitions

Special challenges and leaderboard competitions encourage regular participation.

Referral Programme

Users receive rewards for inviting friends to the platform.

The objective is to develop a habit-forming experience where users return because of competition, rewards and social interaction.

13. Long-Term Vision

The long-term objective of Senevon Games is to establish itself as more than a conventional browser gaming website.

The platform aims to combine:

- Online gaming.
- Multiplayer experiences.
- Social competition.
- Global rankings.
- Virtual rewards.
- Premium gaming experiences.
- Interactive communities.

Simultaneously, Senevon Interactive will explore business opportunities through customized gaming solutions and gamified marketing.

This dual approach will allow Senevon to develop both a consumer-facing gaming platform and a business-oriented gaming solutions vertical.

14. Conclusion

Senevon Games has the potential to establish a sustainable digital entertainment business through a combination of free-to-play gaming, advertising, virtual currency, subscriptions and business partnerships.

The initial focus should be on building a strong gaming experience, acquiring users and improving retention.

Rather than aggressively monetizing users from the beginning, the platform should prioritize engagement, accessibility and community development.

As the user base grows, additional revenue opportunities can be introduced through premium experiences, subscriptions, competitions and B2B gaming solutions.

The ultimate goal is to build an accessible, competitive and engaging gaming ecosystem under the Senevon brand.
